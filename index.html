Manufacturing process

Watch a bottle
come together.

01

Brief & consultation

We agree the fragrance direction, target market, price point, packaging and quantities, and map the timeline to your launch.

02

Formulation & sampling

Perfumers create or match the accord. You receive samples, give feedback and sign off the final formula.

03

Compounding & maceration

Concentrate is blended with perfumer's alcohol and rested so the fragrance rounds out, then chilled and filtered for clarity.

04

Filling, crimping & capping

Bottles are filled to volume, pumps crimped or screwed, collars and caps fitted on our filling lines.

05

Decoration & packing

Labels, screen print or foil, cartons, inserts and cellophane are applied to your packaging specification.

06

Quality control & dispatch

Each batch is checked, coded for traceability and shipped with its documentation, locally or for export.

Quality & compliance

The paperwork buyers ask for, ready on day one.

International buyers judge a manufacturer by its documentation. CASSIE builds compliance into every order instead of chasing it at the end.

  • IFRA

    IFRA-aligned formulas

    Developed within IFRA standards for the product category and destination market.

  • SDS

    Safety data & allergen declarations

    SDS/MSDS, allergen lists and certificates of analysis supplied with your order.

  • LOT

    Batch traceability

    Lot codes on every unit, production records and retained samples.

  • QC

    In-process quality checks

    Component inspection, fill-level, crimp and leak testing, clarity and stability checks.

Questions buyers ask

Private label perfume FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for private label perfume?

MOQs depend on the bottle, cap and box you choose. Stock components allow lower starting quantities; custom moulds and decoration need higher runs. Your quotation confirms exact numbers.

How long does it take to manufacture a perfume line?

Timelines depend on formulation approval and component lead times. Private label with stock packaging is the fastest route; custom fragrance and moulds take longer. We plan backwards from your launch date.

Can you match or recreate a fragrance profile?

Yes. Our perfumers can work from a reference or a written brief to create an original fragrance in the direction you want.

Do you ship perfume internationally?

Yes. CASSIE prepares documentation and packing for export of finished goods and concentrated perfume oils from the UAE.

Can I visit the facility or order samples first?

Absolutely. Request a sample kit or book a manufacturing consultation through the quotation form.

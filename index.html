Manufacturing process

From brief to dispatch, six steps.

  1. 01

    Brief & consultation

    Direction, market, price point, packaging and quantities agreed; timeline planned back from launch.

  2. 02

    Formulation & sampling

    Perfumers create or match the accord. You sample, give feedback and sign off the formula.

  3. 03

    Compounding & maceration

    Concentrate is blended with perfumer's alcohol and rested, then chilled and filtered for clarity.

  4. 04

    Filling, crimping & capping

    Bottles filled to volume, pumps crimped or screwed, collars and caps fitted on our lines.

  5. 05

    Decoration & packing

    Labels, screen print or hot-foil, cartons, inserts and cellophane to your specification.

  6. 06

    Quality control & dispatch

    Each batch checked, lot-coded and shipped with its documents, locally or for export.

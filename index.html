Manufacturing process

Every bottle
starts as a brief.

From brief to dispatch

Six steps.
One accountable partner.

01

Brief & consultation

We agree the fragrance direction, target market, price point, packaging and quantities, and plan backwards from your launch date.

02

Formulation & sampling

Perfumers create or match the accord. You receive samples, give feedback and sign off the final formula.

03

Compounding & maceration

Concentrate is blended with perfumer's alcohol and rested so the fragrance rounds out, then chilled and filtered for clarity.

04

Filling, crimping & capping

Bottles are filled to volume, pumps crimped or screwed, collars and caps fitted on our filling lines.

05

Decoration & packing

Labels, screen print or hot-foil, cartons, inserts and cellophane are applied to your packaging specification.

QC
passed
06

Quality control & dispatch

Each batch is checked, lot-coded for traceability and shipped with its documentation, locally or for export.
